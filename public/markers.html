Room markers

Generating tags…

  • Print at 100% scale (no "fit to page") on plain white paper, one tag per A4 sheet.
  • After printing, measure a tag's black edge: it must be 150 mm. If not, the scale is off and every distance in the room will inherit the error.
  • Mount tags flat (rigid backing, no curl) around the room, roughly at camera height, on more than one wall. Leave the white margin around the black border intact.
  • Tag 0 is the room origin. Mount it upright on a wall: the room frame is x right along the wall, y up, z out of the wall into the room. It anchors the whole map — do not move it after surveying starts.
  • Any position in the room should see at least one tag within ~6 m and not too obliquely (under ~60°).
